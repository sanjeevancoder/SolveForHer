Who is running SolveForHer?

SolveForHer is an independent platform created and solely operated by a 15-year-old boy named Sanjeevan. Driven by his passion for gender equality and his desire to push his personal limits, he manages every single aspect of this platform on his own. From technical development to crafting content, he leads this project independently to build a space dedicated to equal rights and continuous growth.

About Me:

Hi, I’m Sanjeevan. I'm a 15-year-old student developer learning to code on my own, with the goal of becoming a software engineer. Alongside coding, I’m passionate about standing up for gender equality—something inspired by personal experiences from my past. To help drive real change, I recently started a Facebook page called SolveForHer, where I post almost daily about practical ways we can solve gender inequality.

Sanjeevan

Behind the Passion

Everyone has a role model—whether it's an actor, an author, or even a fictional character. My role model is Emma Watson, who served as a UN Women Goodwill Ambassador. Her activism has definitely helped me grow as a person. Above all, I want to emphasize that our past does not define who we are, but our present choices do.
